# Business Cooperation

Contact the maintainer if you need private deployment, custom development, model-provider integration, technical support, or a joint AI image, video, or text workflow solution.

Please include your use case, expected user scale, deployment environment, data and security requirements, integrations, expected delivery scope, timeline, and budget range.

Commercial use is free under the MIT License and does not require a separate license. If you need private delivery or other services, open an issue in this repository describing the intended usage and delivery scope.
